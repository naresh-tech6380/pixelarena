Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 September 2026

PixelArena is a free arcade games website. We don't ask you to sign up, and we don't collect your name, email, or any account details — because there are no accounts.

What we collect

  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used — for example, which pages are visited, which games are played, device and browser type, and approximate location (country/city level). Google Analytics uses cookies for this.
  • On-device storage: Game high scores are saved in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to measure visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the games will still work.

Third parties

  • Google Analytics — website analytics (Google's privacy policy).
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.

In the future we may show ads (e.g. Google AdSense) to keep the games free. Ad providers may use cookies to serve relevant ads.

Children

PixelArena is aimed at players aged 15–20. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Your choices

Use your browser settings to block cookies, or a content blocker to opt out of analytics tracking.

Contact

Questions about this policy: nareshc@karunya.edu.in

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
